# Outcome tracking

Each intervention is linked to a reviewed CMS case, not to a Synthea patient. After an authorized intervention date, calculate outcomes from future claims:

- repeat ED-candidate event within 30, 90, and 180 days;
- outpatient follow-up within 30 days;
- inpatient event within 30 days;
- documented appointment/referral completion.

Do not infer a successful intervention from missing claims. Record unavailable outcomes separately.
